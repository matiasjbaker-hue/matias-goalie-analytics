# Surface brief: GoalieIQ site (landing + signed-in app)

Scope: `index.html` in full. The public landing page is **Persuade**; the signed-in app (Season, Games, Stats, Live Tag, Coach, Admin) is **Operate** and inherits the same world. Audience: goalies (and paying parents) and goalie coaches, equally. Action: create an account / sign in. Proof on hand: the product's own shot map and rebound breakdowns (sample data, labeled), three real testimonials. Constraints: keep every element id, inline handler and JS hook; hero is the user's full-bleed save photo with the headline over it; no generic AI look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GoalieIQ is cut and stitched like a goalie's own custom pads. It refuses the category default (dark navy dashboard, glowing charts, photo-hero + three feature cards) for a world built from pad construction: angled colour panels, quilted channels, stitched seams, a pad-wordmark voice.

OWN-WORLD: White PU face (#F4F5F1) as the ground, pine green (#0F4A35) panels that break at one hard angle, black outer-roll bands (#121412), gold piping (#E8B923) as the single accent and the stitch on primary actions. Dashed stitch rules instead of shadows and card borders; tall channels instead of card grids. Archivo across five width steps: extra-condensed black italic uppercase for wordmark and headings, condensed for metrics (tabular), normal width for body. Save = solid stitch, goal = broken stitch, everywhere a shot appears.

STORY: A visitor sees a real save, reads that every shot gets tagged from their film, sees one sample game broken down exactly as the app does it (shot map, rebound control by body part, GSAx), learns what goalies and coaches each get, hears three goalies, and creates an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The save photo edge to edge, full height. Wordmark top-left, Sign in top-right, both set on the photo. Headline in black condensed italic caps across the lower-left third over calm ice, one supporting line, and a working email field + "Start free" button directly under it. A stitched leader runs from the puck to an example tag label (shot, period, location, xG, result). The photo's bottom edge breaks at one angled pad seam into the pine panel, whose first stitched channel peeks above the fold.

FORM: Custom Pads, position 5 on the grounded list; seed key 4aeab8d3. Raises: stitch-coded results (from Jackfield), stats that unpick to their shots (from Jacquard), photo owns the viewport in quiet (from Fashion flagship), one family at five widths (from Emigre bitmap). Signature interaction: the puck tag stitches itself in (leader dashes draw, then the label lands); motion grammar is short exponential ease-outs, nothing loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
