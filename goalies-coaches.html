Goalies & coaches

Built from the ground up for goaltending, not adapted from a generic stats app. Goalies see their own game shot by shot; coaches see every goalie they develop in one place.

What each side gets

The same tagged shots, read two ways.

For goalies

  • Upload your film, see your statsEvery shot you face gets tracked for you: location, shot quality, rebounds and more.
  • Shot quality and GSAxEach shot is graded for danger, so you see goals saved above expected, not just save percentage.
  • Rebound control by body partGlove, blocker, pads and midsection, so you see exactly where control is being lost.
  • Situations, not just totalsRush chances, screens, breakaways, cross-ice passes and the other high-danger looks.
  • Period by period, game over gameHow your game changes from the first period to the third, and whether it's trending up across the season.
  • Puck playingRims stopped and passes completed, tracked alongside your saves.
  • A report after every gameA PDF with what went well, what to work on and what to practise next.

For coaches

  • Every goalie in one placeEach assigned goalie's performance, trend and last game on one dashboard.
  • The full breakdown for each goalieOpen any goalie to see the same season, game, situation and rebound views they see.
  • Notes that stay with the goalieWrite what you saw and what to work on, right beside the numbers.
  • Shot clipsWatch the clip behind a tagged shot instead of scrubbing through the whole game.
  • AI CoachAsk plain-language questions about a goalie's season and get answers grounded in their tracked data.
  • Game reportsDownload the post-game PDF for any of your goalies' games.

How coaches connect

Coach accounts are connected to their goalies by the GoalieIQ team. Once connected, a coach sees each goalie's games, stats and clips and can leave notes. Goalies who aren't connected to a coach stay private to that coach.

Coaching several goalies? Email goalieiqanalytics@gmail.com and we'll set it up.
