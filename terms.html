About the stats

Stats are produced by tracking each game by hand and by our shot-quality model, which estimates how likely a shot is to score. They're a tool to support development, not a guarantee of results, and small numbers of games can swing a lot. AI features can make mistakes, so use them alongside your own judgement and your coach's.
