Questions

The things goalies, parents and coaches ask before they start. Anything else: goalieiqanalytics@gmail.com.

What does it cost?

New goalie accounts get their first game free. After that you buy games in packs, and each tracked game uses one:

  • 1 game$12
  • 5 games$50
  • 10 games$90
  • 20 games$160

Goalie Plus adds AI Coach and an AI-written analysis in every game report, and covers up to 10 tracked games a month.

What film do I need?

The game video you already have, filmed on a phone or a camera. Full games are fine: uploads go up in parts and pick up where they left off if your connection drops, up to 20 GB per video.

Who tags the shots?

Every shot is tagged from your film by the GoalieIQ team, with AI doing a first pass on shot clips. Location, situation, shot type, release and rebound are all recorded, and any tag can be corrected.

Can my coach see my stats?

Yes, once your coach's account is connected to yours. A connected coach sees your games, stats and clips and can leave notes. Coaches never see goalies they aren't connected to.

Who can see my video?

Only you, a coach connected to you, and the GoalieIQ team. Videos are stored privately and played through links that expire.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Deleting your account from the About tab permanently removes your profile, games, stats, notes and uploaded video, and cancels any subscription.

How to Read This

Rim Stop % measures your ability to get to rimmed pucks and stop them before they continue around the boards. Passes Converted % measures how often your puck touches turn into completed passes. Use both together to evaluate puck-playing execution rather than simply counting touches.
