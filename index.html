Analytics Philosophy

This platform is designed as a development resource rather than simply a statistical résumé.

Traditional goaltending statistics are combined with shot-level, situational and rebound-control data to identify specific areas that can be improved.

The goal is to turn game data into actionable training priorities: identify a weakness, train it, measure it and determine whether it improves.
