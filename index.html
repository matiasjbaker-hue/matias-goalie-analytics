Matias Baker

Matias Baker Goalie Analytics is a personal performance platform designed to track, analyze and visualize goaltending performance throughout the 2026–27 season.

The platform combines traditional goaltending statistics with individual shot-level information to evaluate performance beyond save percentage and goals-against average.

Shot-level analytics currently incorporate distance, location, shot type, rush chances, rebounds, screens, breakaways and cross-ice opportunities.

As the shot database grows, the analytics model can be calibrated using a larger sample of real game situations.

Analytics Methodology

xGA is calculated by assigning every tracked shot an estimated probability of becoming a goal.

Expected SV% represents the save percentage expected from the quality of shots faced.

GSAx is calculated as expected goals against minus actual goals against. A positive number indicates performance above expectation.

Shot grades range from C through A+, with higher grades representing greater estimated danger.
