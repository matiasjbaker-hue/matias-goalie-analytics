About Me

Matias Baker

I'm Matias Baker, a goaltender focused on athleticism, positioning, movement and competing through every situation.

My game is built around reading plays early, staying square to the puck, using efficient movement and positioning, and competing through every situation.

I place a major emphasis on continuously improving my game and understanding exactly where those improvements need to come from.

This website is my personal performance platform. Instead of relying only on traditional statistics, it tracks shot quality, expected goals, rebound control, situational performance, workload and performance trends to give me a much deeper understanding of my game.
