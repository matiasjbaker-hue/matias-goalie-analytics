About

Matias Baker Goalie Analytics is a personal performance platform designed to track and analyze goaltending performance throughout the 2026–27 season with the Pelham Panthers.

The platform goes beyond traditional statistics by breaking down performance by game and by period, allowing trends and areas for improvement to be identified throughout the season.

Advanced shot analytics use individual shot characteristics to estimate shot danger and evaluate goaltending performance beyond traditional save percentage.
