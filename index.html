About Me

I'm Matias Baker, a goaltender with a focus on athleticism, movement and competing through every situation.

My game is built around reading plays early, staying square to the puck and using my skating and positioning to give myself the best chance to make the save. I take pride in being competitive and continually improving the details of my game.

This website is my personal performance platform. I use it to track my games beyond traditional statistics and better understand how I am performing, where I am improving and which areas of my game need more attention.

The goal is simple: use every game as an opportunity to learn, measure progress and become a better goaltender.
