About Me

I'm Matias Baker, a goaltender focused on athleticism, positioning, movement and competing through every situation.

My game is built around reading plays early, staying square to the puck and using efficient movement and positioning to give myself the best chance to make the save. I take pride in being competitive, composed and continually improving the details of my game.

This website is my personal performance platform. It allows me to look beyond traditional statistics and understand how I am performing, where I am improving and which areas of my game need more attention.
