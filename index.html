Analytics Philosophy

This platform is designed as a development resource, not simply as a statistical résumé.

Traditional statistics are combined with shot-level, situational and rebound-control information to understand why performances happen.

The goal is simple: identify → train → measure → adjust.

The most important rule: Analytics identify a pattern. Video identifies the cause. A low SV% in one situation does not automatically mean your technique is wrong. Use the numbers to decide what to investigate, then use video and coaching to decide what to change.
