Analytics Platform

This platform combines traditional goaltending statistics with manually collected shot-level and situational data.

Advanced analysis includes expected save percentage, expected goals against, goals saved above expected and shot-quality classification.

Rebound Control is independently tracked through the goalierebound_control dataset and is not derived from the Shots table.
